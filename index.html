Guide

Registering to Vote

Indian citizens aged 18+ can register online through the Voters' Service Portal.

1

Fill Form 6

Used for new voter registration or shifting constituencies. Available online or via the Voter Helpline App.

2

Verification (BLO)

A Booth Level Officer may visit your home for field verification to confirm your residence.

Pro Tip

Keep your Aadhaar, Proof of Age (Birth Certificate/PAN), and Proof of Residence ready in digital format (under 2MB).

Roadmap

Election Lifecycle Timeline

Notification & Nominations

Official dates are fixed by the ECI. Candidates submit their nomination papers to the Returning Officer, followed by strict scrutiny.

Model Code of Conduct (MCC)

Rules kick in to ensure free and fair campaigning. Ends exactly 48 hours before the polling begins (Silence Period).

Counting & Results

EVM votes are tallied securely. VVPAT slips are randomly matched to ensure absolute transparency before declaring the winner.

Action

Your Experience at the Booth

1

Identity Verification

Present your Voter ID (EPIC) or approved document to the First Polling Officer to verify your name on the electoral roll.

2

Inking & Register

The Second Polling Officer will ink your left forefinger, obtain your signature/thumbprint in the register, and give you a slip.

3

Cast Your Vote (EVM & VVPAT)

Press the blue button on the EVM next to your chosen candidate. Crucial: Look at the VVPAT machine window. A printed slip showing your candidate's serial number, name, and symbol will be visible for exactly 7 seconds before dropping securely into the box.
